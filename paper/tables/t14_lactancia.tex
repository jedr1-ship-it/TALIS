\textit{Notes}: Triple-difference estimates on the full sample. For each exposure-age group, the coefficient of interest is the interaction of living in an affected municipality with being breastfed on 27-F, which compares the gap between affected and unaffected municipalities among the breastfed with the same gap among the children who were not; the column heading names the group reported. Breastfed on 27-F equals one if, according to the mother in 2012, the child was still breastfed in the month of the earthquake, and the regressions include its interaction with each exposure-age group, the exposure-age interactions of equation~(1), and exposure-age, municipality and birth-year fixed effects.
