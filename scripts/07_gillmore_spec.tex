\frametitle{Empirical strategy --- Gillmore (EER, 2026)}
{\footnotesize Difference-in-differences: official earthquake classification
$\times$ birth-cohort exposure to the 2010 Chilean earthquake (27-F).}

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{equation*}
Y_{imtw}=\alpha+\beta\,\bigl(\mathit{Affected}_{it}\times \mathit{Earthquake}_{m}\bigr)
+X_{imt}+\psi_{m}+\theta_{t}+\eta_{w}+e_{imtw}
\tag{1}
\end{equation*}

\vspace{1mm}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1.5pt}\footnotesize
  \item $i$ child, $m$ municipality, $t$ birth cohort, $w$ survey wave (2012, 2017)
  \item $\mathit{Affected}_{it}=1$ if conceived before 27-F: in utero to age~4
        at impact (cohorts 2006--2010, vs.\ conceived after)
  \item $\mathit{Earthquake}_{m}=1$ for officially impacted municipalities
        (Astroza et al.\ 2010: Valpara\'iso, Metropolitana, O'Higgins, Maule,
        Biob\'io, Araucan\'ia); robustness: continuous USGS PGA, distance to
        epicenter, PGA-percentile cutoffs
  \item $X_{imt}$: child, mother and household controls;
        $\psi_{m},\theta_{t},\eta_{w}$: municipality, birth-cohort and wave
        fixed effects; municipality linear trends as robustness
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}
{\footnotesize \textbf{Identification.} $\beta$ = intention-to-treat effect on
exposed cohorts. Parallel trends validated by a cohort event study (joint
$F$-test on 2011--14 placebo cohorts: $p=0.25$); post-quake conceptions
possibly indirectly exposed $\Rightarrow$ $\beta$ is a lower bound. Standard
errors clustered by municipality (145 clusters); national sampling weights.}

\vspace{1.5mm}
{\footnotesize \textbf{Validity.} Placebo and north/south falsifications: null.
Migration between waves 0.78\%
(affected$\rightarrow$unaffected 0.02\%). Fertility, planned births, sex ratio: null in vital records. Differential attrition $+6$~pp $\Rightarrow$
robust to Lee and Kling--Liebman bounds. Romano--Wolf FWER correction:
medium-term Peabody $p=0.02$.}

\vfill
{\tiny Eq.\ (1): Gillmore, \emph{Natural disasters and early child
development: Evidence from an earthquake}, Economics of Education Review 115
(2026) 102817. Preferred: column 3 (no linear trends).}
